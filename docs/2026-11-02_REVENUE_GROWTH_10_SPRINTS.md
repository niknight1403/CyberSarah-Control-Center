# CyberSarah · 10-Sprint Revenue Growth Execution Plan

Goal: improve qualified reach, B2B fit, customer conversion, retention and **verified** recurring revenue. No simulated sales, unauthorized outreach, or bypassing approval gates.

| Sprint | Outcome | Evidence / acceptance gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Elite package works end-to-end in pricing and proposal creation | All package IDs covered by TS checks and targeted tests |
| 2 | Segmented B2B ideal-customer profiles by niche, company size, pains and budget | Persisted persona taxonomy and explicit qualification criteria |
| 3 | Lead-quality scoring with source provenance and deduplication | Reproducible tests; opt-outs excluded |
| 4 | Offer-to-persona matching and differentiated value propositions | Versioned proposals and manual review |
| 5 | Organic reach experiments per channel and content format | UTM links, unique leads and measured conversions |
| 6 | Conversion funnel instrumentation | Visit -> qualified lead -> proposal -> checkout -> paid subscription; no inferred revenue |
| 7 | Consent-aware nurture drafts and re-engagement | No outbound sends without authorization; unsubscribe respected |
| 8 | Checkout failure observability and subscription activation | Verified Stripe webhook and reconciliation tests |
| 9 | Customer retention, renewal and upsell signals | Churn and expansion cohorts measured against real billing events |
| 10 | Revenue dashboard and weekly experiment selection | Verified MRR, refunds, costs, conversion, retention; holdout comparisons |

## Operational constraints
- Automated work may research, segment, draft, measure and recommend.
- No unsolicited mass messages, fabricated testimonials, fake metrics or automatic paid spend.
- External sending, publishing, pricing changes and billing changes require existing approvals.
- Stop/review experiments with low-quality leads or negative contribution margin.
- Definition of done: merged code, passing relevant tests, verified deployment and measured production behavior. A roadmap entry alone is **not** a completed sprint.

## Current progress
- Sprint 1: Elite pricing environment mapping and proposal type integration committed; CI and live validation pending.
- Sprints 2–10: planned, not claimed completed.
